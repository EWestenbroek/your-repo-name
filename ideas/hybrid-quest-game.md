# Hybrid Quest Game

Status: idea

## Problem / Motivation

A video game that blends digital gameplay with real-world quests. The two halves should complement each other without forcing either: you can focus on just the digital side or just the real-world side, but doing both unlocks the full game.

## Rough Approach

- **Two parallel tracks:** a digital track (play at home) and a real-world track (quests done out in the world).
- **Either track works on its own:** each should be a complete, satisfying experience on its own terms.
- **Both together give the full game:** combining them unlocks extra content, story, abilities or areas that neither track has alone.
- **Genre (leaning):** multiplayer RPG. Not decided yet.

## Open Questions

- Multiplayer RPG, or something else? Co-op, competitive, or MMO-style?
- What counts as a "real-world quest"? Location-based (GPS), physical activity, puzzles/scavenger hunts, social tasks, creative tasks?
- How is real-world completion verified? GPS, QR codes/NFC tags, photos, honor system?
- How do the tracks feed each other? Real-world progress gives digital loot/stats? Digital story unlocks real-world quests?
- How do you keep "full game requires both" from feeling like a penalty for solo-track players?
- Safety and accessibility of real-world quests (location, mobility, weather, privacy).
- Platform: mobile only, or PC/console paired with a mobile companion app?
